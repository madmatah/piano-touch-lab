## Reference data

### David C. Stanwood's standard curves

- **What:** the standard Front Weight curves, levels 5 to 9, and the standard Strike Weight curves, levels 1 to 13, one value per key of an 88-key keyboard, as David C. Stanwood lists them in his articles on touch weight metrology. The curves the interface draws are rebuilt from five keypoints per level, at keys 1, 22, 44, 66 and 88, within 0.1 g of his values: for the front weight, a formula of the level, which the design also extends to levels 1 to 4 and 10 to 12, beyond the levels his articles list; for the strike weight, the keypoints of each level.
- **Where:** `src/lib/piano/touch-design/data/front-weights.ts`, `src/lib/piano/touch-design/data/strike-weight.ts` and `src/lib/piano/touch-design/generate-custom-front-weight-keypoints.ts`.
- **From:** David C. Stanwood's articles, and the technical documents of his [Piano Technicians Resource Page](https://www.stanwoodpiano.com/touchweight.htm).
- **Terms:** published reference values, reproduced with credit to their author.
